NEPAL / FILM / MOUNTAINS

About.

Expedition cinematographer, Mountain Guide from Nepal.

I'm Sunil Tamang, an expedition cinematographer and mountaineer based in Nepal. I spend much of my time in the mountains, both climbing and documenting the people, places, and stories that unfold along the way.

My experience in high-altitude expeditions and technical climbing shapes how I approach filmmaking. Being part of the journey allows me to capture moments as they happen — the challenges, the quiet moments, and everything in between.

Alongside filmmaking, I'm an aspirant mountain guide with NNMGA, working toward IFMGA certification. Whether behind the camera or on the mountain, I'm driven by a love for exploration, meaningful storytelling, and the places that have shaped my life.
