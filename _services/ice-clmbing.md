####################################################

faq:

  - question: I've never ice climbed before. Can I join?

    answer: >
      Absolutely. Beginner experiences start with the fundamentals and
      progress at a pace that matches your experience and confidence.

  - question: How physically fit do I need to be?

    answer: >
      You should be comfortable hiking in mountain terrain and spending
      several hours outdoors. The exact physical demands depend on the
      route and conditions.

  - question: What equipment do I need?

    answer: >
      A complete equipment list will be provided before the climb.
      Technical equipment can be arranged or rented where available.

  - question: What happens if the ice or weather conditions are bad?

    answer: >
      Mountain conditions always come first. If conditions are unsafe,
      the route, timing or objective may be changed. Safety is more
      important than completing a particular route.

  - question: Can I book a private climb?

    answer: >
      Yes. Experiences are designed for very small private groups,
      allowing the day to be adapted to your goals and ability.

  - question: Where do we meet?

    answer: >
      The meeting point depends on the selected climbing area and
      itinerary. Full details will be provided during trip planning.

  - question: Can photographers join?

    answer: >
      Yes. Photography can be incorporated into the experience and
      the itinerary can be adapted around documentation when required.

---

Ice climbing is more than reaching the top of a frozen waterfall.

It is the sound of an ice tool finding its first placement, the feeling of crampons gripping blue ice and the quiet concentration that comes with moving vertically through a winter mountain.

Whether you're taking your first swing or preparing for more technical alpine objectives, each experience is built around learning, movement and time on the mountain.

You climb. You learn. You experience the ice.

And hopefully, you leave already thinking about the next route.
